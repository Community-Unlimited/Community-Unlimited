02 / What we are for

What we are for

There is work here.

Boon Lay does not need another place to sit. It needs people who can run a morning.

Rosters, teams, budgets, a difficult customer before nine in the morning. A Saturday walk, a coffee counter and a Wednesday afternoon each need someone steady holding them together. It is not a job, and it still has to be done properly.

Contribute, don’t attend.

You come in to do something, not to be entertained. Pull the coffee, set the pace on the walk, keep the table talking.

If friendships come, they come from working beside people. That was true at work too.

The skill outlasted the job.

Years of running shifts, chairing meetings, calming somebody down before the queue noticed.

You might come to slow down. You might come because stopping did not suit you. Either one is useful here.

One door, and it is WhatsApp.

No fees and nothing to buy. You message us, we send you where to meet, you turn up. We take your name at the session, and that is the whole of the paperwork.

The meeting points come to you by message because they are still being confirmed.

No screening at the door.

Retired, half retired, or still working with the end of it in sight. Nobody is turned away for what they did before, or asked what they used to earn.

The Saturday walk is forty minutes and then breakfast. Start there.

03 / The pathway

Five steps, in order

From first message to running the morning.

You move when someone who has worked beside you says you are ready.

  1. STEP 01

    Register

    Message us on WhatsApp and tell us your name. We send back where to meet. Turn up without messaging and you are still welcome.

  2. STEP 02

    Train

    Training happens while you work, not in a classroom. On the coffee counter that is your first two shifts: the machine, the milk, the order things happen in.

  3. STEP 03

    Qualify

    Someone who has stood beside you signs off that you can hold the counter on your own. A person decides that, not software. There are no exams.

  4. STEP 04

    Deploy

    Now the morning is yours to run. You open the counter, you keep it moving, you make the calls that need making. The people who come in after you learn by watching.

  5. STEP 05

    Lead

    The senior roles are real. The titles are not decided yet. The first people through will name them, then hold them.

How long it takes depends on how often you come.
